## 1. General Podcast Details

* **Podcast Name:** The Secret Garden - Graded Audiobook Series (TKPROF.AI)
* **Description:**
  Classical literature is one of the best ways to build vocabulary and language skills. However, archaic 19th-century grammar and outdated syntax often break the flow. 

  This series presents Frances Hodgson Burnett's 1911 masterpiece—fully modernized for English as a Second Language (ESL) learners, students, and modern readers. Enjoy the original story, characters, and emotional depth without having to look up archaic Victorian words.

  Includes both the Graded Modern English version and the AI-assisted 2026 Modern Korean Translation.
