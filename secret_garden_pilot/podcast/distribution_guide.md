# Step-by-Step Podcast Distribution Guide

This guide details how to host, upload, and distribute your modernized *The Secret Garden Chapter 1* episodes using **Spotify for Podcasters** (completely free, standard distributor).

---

## Part 1: Setting Up Your Account & Podcast Channel

1. **Visit the Platform:**
   Go to [https://podcasters.spotify.com/](https://podcasters.spotify.com/) and click **Sign up**.
   * *Tip:* If you already have a personal Spotify account, you can log in with it, or create a new dedicated business email login.

2. **Select Hosting Option:**
   Choose **"I want to start a podcast"** (this sets up free Spotify hosting).

3. **Fill in Podcast Profile Settings:**
   * **Podcast Name:** `The Secret Garden - Graded Audiobook Series (TKPROF.AI)`
   * **Podcast Description:** (Copy description from [podcast_metadata.md](file:///D:/git_repo/thefirstaicompany/secret_garden_pilot/podcast/podcast_metadata.md))
   * **Category:** Choose `Education` and subcategory `Language Learning`.
   * **Language:** `English`

4. **Upload Cover Art:**
   Upload [podcast_cover.png](file:///D:/git_repo/thefirstaicompany/secret_garden_pilot/podcast/podcast_cover.png) (3000x3000px).

## Part 4: Auto-Distribution to Other Platforms (Apple & Amazon)

Once your first episode is live on Spotify, a public RSS Feed is generated.

1. **Find your RSS Feed Link:**
   * Go to **Settings** > **Podcast settings** inside Spotify for Podcasters.
   * Scroll down to find the URL labeled **RSS Feed** (e.g., `https://anchor.fm/s/xxxxxx/podcast/rss`). Copy this link.

2. **Apple Podcasts Distribution (Automatic or Direct Submission):**
   * *Option A (Automatic):* Spotify for Podcasters has a toggle to auto-submit your feed to Apple Podcasts.
   * *Option B (Recommended for fast approval):* Go to [Apple Podcasts Connect](https://podcastsconnect.apple.com/), sign in with an Apple ID, click **Add Show (+)**, paste your copied RSS feed link, and click **Submit**. (Usually approved in 24-48 hours).

3. **Amazon Music & Others:**
   * Copy the RSS feed link and submit it directly to [Amazon Music for Podcasters](https://podcasters.amazon.com/) to expand reach.
